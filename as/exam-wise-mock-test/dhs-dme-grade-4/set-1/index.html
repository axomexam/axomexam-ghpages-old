    Q7. A man sold two articles for Rs. 990 each. On one he gains 10% and on the other he loses 10%. His overall result is:
    (A) No profit no loss লাভ-লোকচান নাই
    (B) 1% profit 1% লাভ
    (C) 1% loss 1% লোকচান
    (D) 2% loss 2% লোকচান
    1% লোকচান
    Answer: 1% loss
    একে মূল্যত আৰু সমান শতাংশ লাভ-ক্ষতিত দুটা বস্তু বিক্ৰী কৰিলে সদায় (10²/100) = 1% ক্ষতি হয়।
    Explanation: When two articles are sold at the same price with equal percentage gain and loss, there is always a loss of (10²/100) = 1%.

    (C) start শুৰু কৰা
    (D) commence আৰম্ভ কৰা
    শেষ কৰা
    Answer: finish

    Q14. A boat goes 16 km downstream in 2 hours and 8 km upstream in 2 hours. Speed of the stream is:
    (A) 2 km/h 2 কিমি/ঘণ্টা
    (B) 4 km/h 4 কিমি/ঘণ্টা
    (C) 3 km/h 3 কিমি/ঘণ্টা
    (D) 6 km/h 6 কিমি/ঘণ্টা
    2 কিমি/ঘণ্টা
    Answer: 2 km/h
    সোঁতেৰ গতি = 16/2 = 8 কি.মি./ঘণ্টা আৰু উজান = 8/2 = 4 কি.মি./ঘণ্টা; সোঁতৰ বেগ = (8 − 4)/2 = 2 কি.মি./ঘণ্টা।
    Explanation: Downstream = 16/2 = 8 km/h and upstream = 8/2 = 4 km/h; stream speed = (8 − 4)/2 = 2 km/h.

    Answer: Bansuri (flute)

    Q50. The cat jumped ___ the fence.
    (A) on
    (B) into
    (C) over
    (D) at
    Answer: over

    Explanation: 'Over' means across from one side to the other, which fits jumping across a fence.
