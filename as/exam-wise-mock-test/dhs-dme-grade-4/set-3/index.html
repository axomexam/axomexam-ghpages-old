    (A) 48 km/h 48 কিমি/ঘণ্টা

    (C) 45 km/h 45 কিমি/ঘণ্টা
    (D) 52 km/h 52 কিমি/ঘণ্টা
    48 কিমি/ঘণ্টা
    Answer: 48 km/h
    মাধ্য বেগ = 2xy/(x + y) = (2 × 40 × 60)/100 = 48 কি.মি./ঘণ্টা।
    Explanation: Average speed = 2xy/(x + y) = (2 × 40 × 60)/100 = 48 km/h.

    Q8. Two pipes can fill a tank in 10 hours and 15 hours. A third pipe can empty it in 30 hours. If all three are opened, the tank will be filled in:
    (A) 7.5 hours 7.5 ঘণ্টা
    (B) 8 hours 8 ঘণ্টা
    (C) 6 hours 6 ঘণ্টা
    (D) 9 hours 9 ঘণ্টা
    7.5 ঘণ্টা
    Answer: 7.5 hours
    নিট হাৰ = 1/10 + 1/15 − 1/30 = 4/30 = 2/15; সময় = 15/2 = 7.5 ঘণ্টা।
    Explanation: Net rate = 1/10 + 1/15 − 1/30 = 4/30 = 2/15; time = 15/2 = 7.5 hours.

    Q9. The curved surface area of a cylinder of radius 7 cm and height 10 cm is: (Take π = 22/7)
    (A) 440 cm² 440 ছেমি²
    (B) 220 cm² 220 ছেমি²
    (C) 154 cm² 154 ছেমি²
    (D) 308 cm² 308 ছেমি²
    440 ছেমি²
    Answer: 440 cm²

    Q23. A train 120 m long running at 60 km/h takes 30 seconds to cross a platform. The length of the platform is:
    (A) 360 m 360 মিটাৰ
    (B) 380 m 380 মিটাৰ
    (C) 400 m 400 মিটাৰ
    (D) 420 m 420 মিটাৰ
    380 মিটাৰ
    Answer: 380 m
