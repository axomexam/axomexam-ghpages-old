    Q1. If a man walks at 4 km/h, he is 10 minutes late. If he walks at 5 km/h, he is 5 minutes early. The required time to reach on time is:
    (A) 55 minutes 55 মিনিট
    (B) 65 minutes 65 মিনিট
    (C) 45 minutes 45 মিনিট
    (D) 50 minutes 50 মিনিট
    65 মিনিট
    Answer: 65 minutes
    দূৰত্ব d আৰু প্ৰয়োজনীয় সময় t হ'লে d/4 = t + 10 আৰু d/5 = t − 5 (মিনিটত); সমাধান কৰি t = 65 মিনিট।
    Explanation: If distance is d and required time is t, then d/4 = t + 10 and d/5 = t − 5 (in minutes); solving gives t = 65 minutes.

    • 50 questions drawn from every subject of the DHS & DME Grade IV paper.
    • Four options per question, with a single correct answer.
    • Bilingual questions, options and explanations.
    • Instant scoring and a full answer review on submit.

    1. Pick your question language.
